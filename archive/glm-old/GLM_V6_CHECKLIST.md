# GLM v6 visual QA checklist

For every production beat:

- Can a first-time viewer name the current chapter without searching?
- Is the current hero object obvious within one second?
- Is all required teaching text comfortably readable at 1920×1080 and at the user's 1904×950 viewport?
- Does every visible fact serve the current beat or deliberately set up a later one?
- If a fact is future-useful, is it visually quieter than the current idea?
- Does the same conceptual object keep its identity across transitions?
- Does animation explain identity, cause, hierarchy, scale, or a change of conceptual world?
- Can the viewer understand the beat with narration plus the visual, without reading a paragraph?
- Are controls next to the object/action they affect?
- Are keyboard, repeat-click, reduced-motion and reverse-navigation states safe?
- Does the frame remain fully inside the recording safe area at wide and short viewport sizes?
- Does the page feel like an illustrated lesson rather than generic website UI?
