# Part 01 — Opening Show v2

This file is the visual contract for the opening. The spoken Tell remains in `01-opening-headache.md`.

## Rule for this version

The narration asks the questions. The canvas does not.

Do not write:

- “Then what is all this for?”
- “Just keep these?”
- “LOAD ONLY SELECTED?”
- “What stops this?”
- explanatory paragraphs about MoE, RAM, weights, or routing

The viewer should infer the puzzle from scale, motion, persistence, and contrast.

Minimal labels are allowed only when they name an object or number that would otherwise be ambiguous: `GLM-5.3-Flash`, `320B`, `~18B`, `MoE`, `router`, `weights`, `GB`.

---

## Visual grammar

The same actors persist through the opening:

- **orange dot** = the token we follow
- **small squares** = parameter/expert capacity
- **orange squares** = currently active path
- **large physical rack** = stored model weights
- **round junction** = router
- **nine-block working set** = eight routed experts plus the shared expert

Do not introduce these as a legend slide. Their meaning should be learned by watching what they do.

---

## Beat 1 — Establish the object

Show GLM-5.3-Flash and a huge field made from many tiny learned-value marks.

`320B` sits beside it as the only hero number.

The visual job is scale, not architecture.

---

## Beat 2 — One token changes the field

One orange token enters.

Only a tiny slice of the field becomes orange.

`~18B` appears beside the active slice.

The viewer has already seen the difference before the narration defines “active”.

---

## Beat 3 — Let both truths coexist

Keep the orange path active while the quiet majority remains physically present.

Do not replace the 320B field with an 18B diagram.

The contradiction comes from seeing a tiny working region inside a much larger owned object.

---

## Beat 4 — Make the unused-looking mass dominate

The active region becomes visually small.

The quiet field gets the visual weight again: more area, more depth, more presence.

A simple brace/gesture may point at the majority, but no written question.

---

## Beat 5 — The same mass becomes experts

The parameter field reorganizes into a wall of expert blocks.

This should feel like the same capacity gaining structure, not a new slide arriving.

Only after the wall is readable may a tiny `MoE` mark appear.

---

## Beat 6 — Few wake up

Exactly eight routed expert blocks become orange.

The separate shared expert becomes teal.

The other experts remain visible and quiet.

A tiny `8 — 1` count is enough; narration explains what it means.

---

## Beat 7 — Reveal the physical storage behind the experts

Without cutting, introduce a large rack of stored weight blocks beside/behind the expert field.

The selected experts keep glowing.

Crucially, the rack does not shrink when most experts go inactive.

This is the second headache, created entirely by the picture.

Use only tiny object labels such as `weights` and `GB` if needed for orientation.

---

## Beat 8 — Let the obvious idea form visually

Copies of the eight selected experts plus the shared expert peel away from the full wall and collect into a small nine-block working set.

The full weight rack stays enormous.

No thought bubble. No sentence.

The small set itself is the thought.

---

## Beat 9 — The router earns its entrance

Now add the router between the token and expert field.

Token enters the round junction.

Eight clean rays survive toward the selected experts.

Only after the behavior is obvious may the tiny word `router` appear inside the junction.

---

## Beat 10 — Build the viewer's hypothetical machine

Move the router and the nine-block working set toward each other.

The full expert field becomes secondary.

The large weight rack still exists on the opposite side.

Visually, the viewer is constructing the same idea the narration proposes: router + only the work that was selected.

Do not show failure yet.

---

## Beat 11 — Make that hypothetical system feel temptingly small

A compact machine shell forms around the nine-block set.

The router sits immediately beside it.

The resulting active system should look dramatically smaller than the 320B storage rack behind it.

This beat should make the proposed optimization feel reasonable, not foolish.

---

## Beat 12 — Hold the incompatible scales

Do not add a question headline.

Freeze the composition with:

- tiny token
- router
- tiny active machine
- enormous ghosted weight store

Let the viewer stare at those two scales while the narration asks what is stopping us.

The image should feel unresolved without using a written question mark.

---

## Beat 13 — Refuse the architecture dump visually

A dense 45-layer architecture sheet briefly enters.

The token stays visually cleaner and more important than the sheet.

Then the sheet slides/folds away while the token remains.

The narration says why a final architecture diagram is not the way to learn this.

---

## Beat 14 — Follow the actor, not the diagram

A deep arched entrance opens.

The token travels into it.

No headline overlaps the token.

Ideally no headline is necessary at all: movement into the doorway is the transition.

The next section should feel like the camera followed the same actor into the model rather than loading a new webpage.

---

## Final test

Mute the narration and click through beats 1–14.

A first-time viewer should still be able to say approximately:

> Huge model. One token only lights a small part. The huge capacity is many experts. Only a few experts work. But all those weights are still sitting there. A router chooses the few. Those few could fit in something much smaller. So why is the huge stored model still relevant? Now we are following the token inside to find out.

If they cannot infer that, fix the objects/motion before adding more text.
