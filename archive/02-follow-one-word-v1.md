## The local hook

A transition section is the easiest place in a video to lose someone, so this
one is not allowed to be a corridor. It has its own surprise, and it is a good
one: **the thing that picks the experts never sees the word at all.**

That is true — routing reads the current representation, never the raw text —
and it reframes everything §1 established. The viewer thought they understood
the router. They did not.
