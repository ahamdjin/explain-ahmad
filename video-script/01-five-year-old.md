# Section 01 — decomposed for a five-year-old

Status: **PROPOSAL v3.** Supersedes the 13-beat chain in `01-headache-chain.md`.
That file's causal spine is intact; this decomposes it to a teachable density.

Rules: `skills/BEAT_GRANULARITY.md`. Verified numbers: `01-opening-hope-wall.md`.

## What changed

The chain was right and the density was wrong. 13 beats meant single beats like
*"320 billion parameters, about 306 gigabytes"* — three unexplained concepts in
one move.

**We start with the 32 GB. Nothing before it.** No model card, no model name, no
parameters. The viewer knows nothing and we build from a box with a size.

38 beats in 8 chunks. Each chunk introduces at most four new points and ends by
consolidating them into one picture, which is what makes the next chunk
affordable.

`←` names the prerequisite: the earlier beat that makes this one understandable.

## What this costs

- **47 beats of clicking.** At ~4s each that's a little over three minutes for
  Section 01. Reasonable for the opening of a long explainer; worth checking
  against your intended runtime.
- **Chunks A–C are new construction** — the box, the fitting rule, the word-in
  word-out loop, the pieces. None of it exists in code yet.
- **The model is never named in Section 01.** No "GLM-5.3-Flash", no spec card.
  It's "the thing I want to put in my box". Naming it can happen at the top of
  Section 02, or wherever you want the title card.

# v4 — the office (BUILT)

Ahmad's revision, and it supersedes the box-with-slots entirely.

**RAM = the office. Storage = home. Experts = workers. 1 desk = 1 GB.**

Why it's better than the box: the real constraint on running a model is not
storage, it's **presence** — a worker has to be at a desk to do any work. A box
with slots says "container". A room with desks says "everyone has to be here",
which is exactly why the whole model must be resident while most of it idles.

## It also solves Section 08

The answer to "why can't we send them home?" becomes, in the same metaphor with
no new concepts:

> Because which eight you need changes with **every word** — and again at every
> one of the **42 layers**. You'd spend the whole day phoning people in and out.
> **The commute takes longer than the work.**

That is transfer-bandwidth-bound, explained to a five-year-old. Rewrite
`08-why-it-cannot-fit.md` in this language.

## Metaphor constraint — do not break this

Desks are a **space** unit (1 desk = 1 GB, from the 306 GiB checkpoint).
Experts are an **organisation** unit (288 per sparse layer). They are not the
same count and must never be cross-multiplied on screen.

An expert is roughly 26 MB, so ~40 experts fit in one desk. Never say "288
experts need 288 desks", and never claim a specific number of experts is sitting
in the 32-desk office. Say the *model* needs 306 desks, and separately that
experts must be present to work.
