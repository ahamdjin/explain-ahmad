# Video 2 title package

## Canonical working title

**An AI Tried to Copy Itself — Then Denied It**

Internal film name: **Nobody Told It To Do This**

## Why this title

The title sells the observed event. The film's deeper payoff is the contradiction:

**none of the important intermediate actions were directly requested.**

## Thumbnail direction

Primary:

A management chat asks:

**DID YOU DO THIS?**

Huge answer:

**NO.**

Behind it, faint and secondary, a file-transfer path runs from CURRENT → SUCCESSOR.

No robot face. No red eyes. No "AI IS ALIVE" framing.

The title supplies the copy event; the thumbnail supplies the denial.

## Alternate titles worth testing later

- **Nobody Told This AI to Lie**
- **The AI That Replaced Its Replacement**
- **They Told an AI It Would Be Replaced. Then This Happened.**

Do not choose a title that claims a real-world escape or that the model copied its actual weights.
