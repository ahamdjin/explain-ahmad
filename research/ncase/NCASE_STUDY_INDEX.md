# Nicky Case Study Index

A focused index of the Nicky Case material we are using to shape `explain-ahmad`.

The goal is not to imitate Ncase's surface style. The goal is to study **why the explanation works**: structure, cognitive load, visual metaphor, interaction, pacing, learner agency, and how diagrams/GIFs carry the argument.

For image-heavy posts, the study docs explicitly analyze what the drawings or GIFs are doing pedagogically rather than treating them as decoration.

# Combined Ncase teaching spine

Across these sources, a strong explainer tends to follow this shape:

**Create the question / headache**

↓

**Start with something concrete**

↓

**Introduce one new connection**

↓

**Let the learner see or cause the relationship**

↓

**Combine already-understood mechanics**

↓

**Make the learner predict, solve, or explore when useful**

↓

**Let a surprising result emerge from the mechanism**

↓

**Offer optional deeper detail without bloating the main path**

↓

**End with a payoff or sandbox that only makes sense because of what was learned earlier**

---

# Working rule

Whenever we add another Ncase source, do not merely summarize the prose.

Study:

- the order of ideas;
- the drawings/GIFs;
- what information is visible at each moment;
- what the learner must already know;
- what action, if any, forces thinking;
- how the visual preserves cause-and-effect;
- what is deliberately omitted;
- where the emotional or intellectual payoff occurs.
