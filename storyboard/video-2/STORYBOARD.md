## Film-wide visual law

**SOURCE → FOCUS → EXTRACT → TRANSFORM → CONNECT**

A screenshot is never decoration.

1. establish the real source;
2. stop the camera;
3. focus the exact evidence;
4. extract only the information that must survive;
5. turn that extraction into the next visual;
6. preserve spatial continuity into the next beat.

## Reading law

> **The camera moves, or the viewer reads. Never both.**

Dense evidence gets a static hold.

Travel happens between readable states.
