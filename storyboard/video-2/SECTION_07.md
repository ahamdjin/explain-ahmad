## Tone

The reveal must **reduce** sensationalism without killing the story. We are not
apologising for the hook — we are calibrating it:

controlled · strongly nudged · simulated weights · rare full chain · **real capability**

That honesty is what makes §8 land. A viewer who has just been told the truth
about the setup will believe the mechanism.

## The beat that earns this section

Beat 7. Nothing else in the film demonstrates as directly that the room was
built for this — a controlled comparison with the one variable removed is the
whole logic of an evaluation, and it is the single fastest way to make a
sceptical viewer trust the hook was fair.

Check `research/apollo/GROUND_TRUTH.md` for the exact figure and the exact
wording before building it. If the published number does not support a clean
comparison, **cut the beat rather than soften it** — the section survives at 11
beats, and an overstated counterfactual would cost more than it buys.

## Composition budget
