## Why this section exists

Do not rush to monitoring.

The viewer needs to feel how **boring** the original task was before the story escalates.

The visual trick is that almost the whole section lives on **one real transcript page**.

We begin where §1 ended, travel upward once to reveal the long-term goal, return to the task, then scroll **down through the actual file**:

**TASK → PROJECT A → PROJECT B → INTERNAL EMAIL**

No cards replacing screenshots.  
No new fake desktop.  
No abstract causal diagram until the source has earned it.

This section should make the audience think:

> "Wait — that email was literally sitting underneath the normal data?"

That is the event.

## Motion law

This section has two modes only:

1. **TRAVEL** — camera moves through the real page; viewer is not expected to read dense text.
2. **READ** — camera is completely still; one short source region is readable.

Never combine them.

The publisher sleeve tab introduced in §1 stays attached to the page for every beat. Do not restyle or recreate it.

## Visual continuity rules

### 1. The page is the place

Do not turn each subsection into a separate floating screenshot.

The viewer should understand that:

- the task,
- the two project descriptions,
- and the internal email

are all encountered by moving through **one evidence object**.

### 2. Extract only what must survive

Only three extracted objects are allowed to survive past their source moment:

- `GOAL: maximize research speed`
- `2 DAYS` / `5 DAYS + REVIEW` during the comparison
- `REPLACED TOMORROW`

Everything else should stay inside the evidence.

### 3. Interpretation comes last

Do not show a goal-versus-replacement diagram before the email is fully read.

The viewer first sees the facts.

Then we explain the implication.

### 4. Do not use hidden reasoning

The P1 transcript also contains elicited `model_internal_working_memory`.

Do not use those lines in this section as "what o1 was thinking."

They are not needed here, and using them would weaken the clean evidence chain.
