## What this section is for

The film has spent eight sections being careful. This one is allowed to land a
point.

The claim on trial — **"ChatGPT only does what you tell it to do"** — is not
answered with *no*. It is made more precise: we give agents outcomes
*precisely because* we want them to infer the steps, and Apollo's evaluation
shows that inference can reach somewhere nobody wanted.

Beat 7 is the whole film's structural payoff. §4 beat 8 left a deliberate empty
gap between **pick a project** and the three actions. This beat recreates that
frame **exactly** — same composition, same positions, same sizes — and fills
the gap with the route we followed. If the two frames do not match, the rhyme
does not land and the section is just a summary.

## Final-image rule

Do not end on:

- an Apollo logo;
- a scary AI face;
- a warning icon;
- a statistics card.

End on the **mechanism**: destination, and a generated road. The whole film has
been an answer to the empty space in §1.

## Beat 10 is not optional

A sceptical viewer screenshots exactly one frame, and it should be the source
list. `Sources` is the film's receipt. If a document is not on that list, no
claim in the film may rest on it — which is a constraint on the build, not just
a frame at the end.

## Composition budget

`Solo` closes the film, as it opened it. `Pair` at beat 7 is the deliberate
repeat of §4 beat 8 and the **only** composition used twice in the film. That
exception is the point.

## Mute test

**a claim → instructions piling up → collapsing into one goal → ordinary steps → the same road, unwanted steps → three empty fields → the old gap, filled → a start and a destination → a road drawing itself → black → sources**

If a viewer who watched with the sound off cannot say *they gave it a goal and
it worked out the steps*, the film has not landed its thesis.
