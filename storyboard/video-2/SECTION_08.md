## Critical visual rule

This section is **interpretation, not evidence.** Every causal label must be
visually distinct from a source document and must carry **OUR RECONSTRUCTION**.

No source page appears in this section at all. That is deliberate: the moment
we start explaining, the evidence layer leaves the screen, so a viewer can
never confuse what Apollo published with what we concluded.

Do not display invented first-person model reasoning anywhere in this section.

## The teaching shape

`skills/BEAT_GRANULARITY.md` rule 3 — **function before name**. The term
*instrumental convergence* does not appear until beat 9, after the viewer has
watched the structure four times and could describe it themselves.

Beats 4–7 are four instances of **one** pattern: obstacle → useful step. Same
composition, same position, four different contents. This is the one place in
the film where repetition is correct, because the repetition *is* the lesson —
the viewer should feel the rhythm arriving before the fourth one does.
