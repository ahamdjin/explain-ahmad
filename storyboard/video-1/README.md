## The chapter wall, in one line

Every section **opens by banking the previous section's answer in one clause**,
then adds its **but**; and **closes by naming the mechanism as finished** and
saying what is now missing. No title cards — Ahmad's call, and it is also what
*The Evolution of Trust* does. `STORY_SPINE.md` §5 has the table for all
thirteen.
